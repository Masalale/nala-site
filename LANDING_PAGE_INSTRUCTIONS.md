## Strategic Notes

### DTC Focus Priorities
1. **Customer Acquisition**: Instagram/Facebook ads targeting "natural skincare Kenya"
2. **Retention**: Monthly subscription model (predictable revenue)
3. **AOV Growth**: Bundle offers (Complete Collection performs best)
4. **Trust Building**: Before/after photos, ingredient transparency

### Inventory Management
- **Batch size**: 20 bars (as stated)
- **Curing time**: 4-6 weeks (plan production accordingly)
- **Reorder point**: When inventory drops to 10 bars per type
- **Seasonal planning**: Increase production 6 weeks before holidays

### Marketing Priorities
1. **Instagram** (Primary): Product photography, behind-the-scenes, customer features
2. **WhatsApp Business**: Customer service, order updates, quick sales
3. **Word of Mouth**: Referral program (Give 100 KES, Get 100 KES)
4. **Influencer partnerships**: Micro-influencers (5k-20k followers) in wellness/beauty niche

### Future Additions (Phase 2)
- Loyalty points program
- Seasonal limited editions
- Gift sets with packaging
- Wholesale inquiry form (even if not actively pursuing)
- Blog: "NALA Journal" with skincare tips
- Affiliate/referral tracking system

### Success Metrics to Track
- Conversion rate (target: 2-3%)
- Average Order Value (target: 1,800+ KES)
- Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)
- Repeat purchase rate (target: 40%+ within 90 days)
- Cart abandonment rate (target: <70%)

### Tech Stack Recommendations
Based on this baseline, the design-dna-generator will work well with:
- **Framework**: Next.js (SEO-friendly, fast)
- **E-commerce**: Shopify Lite or WooCommerce integration
- **Payments**: M-Pesa API + manual bank transfer
- **Hosting**: Vercel or Netlify (fast CDN for Kenya)
- **Forms**: Typeform or native forms
- **Email**: Mailchimp or Sendy (self-hosted, cheaper for Kenya)

### Cost Analysis
- **Production cost**: 420 KES/bar
- **Selling price**: 650-750 KES/bar
- **Gross margin**: 35-44% per bar
- **Break-even**: Need to sell X bars/month to cover fixed costs

**Note**: Margin is healthy but not huge. Focus on:
1. Reducing production costs at scale
2. Increasing AOV through bundles
3. Subscription model for predictable revenue
4. Reducing delivery costs through pickup option
